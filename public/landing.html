Built for the awkward parts

Ties break on time

Two people on full marks is a draw everywhere else. Here the faster attempt wins, so your leaderboard always has a top.

A timer that cannot be gamed

The countdown runs on the server. Refreshing resumes the same attempt with the same deadline; it never hands out a fresh clock.

Nothing lost to a closed tab

Answers save as they are picked. If the time runs out, the attempt is submitted automatically with whatever was answered.

Answers stay on the server

Participants are sent the questions, never the answer key. Scoring happens server-side, and the leaderboard never leaks which option was right.

Two-factor for makers

Maker accounts use a password plus an authenticator app, with single-use recovery codes. Takers need no account at all.

Runs on your own machine

No database to install, no accounts with anyone else, no data leaving your network. Start it, share the address, done.
